Verimed Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 28, 2026 · Website: https://verimed.app · Contact: verimed.app@gmail.com

Verimed ("we", "us") is a study platform for students preparing for the Saudi Medical Licensing Examination (SMLE). This policy explains what data we collect, why, and how you can control it.

1. Data we collect

  • Account data: your name, email address and (optionally) gender, which you give us when you register. If you sign in with Google, we receive only your name, email address and Google account ID from Google. We do not receive your Google password and we do not access your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data.
  • Study data: the questions you submit, the answers we return, your saved items, reports you send about an answer, and your mock-exam results.
  • Payment data: payments are processed by a licensed third-party payment gateway. We receive only the payment status, amount and a reference number. We never see or store your card number.
  • Technical data: basic usage logs (time, page, errors) and data stored in your browser's local storage to keep you signed in.

2. How we use your data

  • To create and secure your account and let you sign in.
  • To answer and verify your study questions and keep your history.
  • To manage your package, payments and access period.
  • To send you account emails (sign-in codes, receipts) and, occasionally, offers about Verimed. You can unsubscribe from offers at any time.
  • To fix errors and improve answer quality (for example, reviewing reported answers).

We do not sell your personal data and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Sharing

We share data only with service providers that run the platform for us: cloud hosting, email delivery, the payment gateway, and AI processing services that analyze the question text you submit. They may use the data only to provide their service to us. We may disclose data if required by law.

4. Google user data

Verimed's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google sign-in data (name, email) is used only to create and sign in to your Verimed account.

5. Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can ask us to delete your account and data at any time by emailing verimed.app@gmail.com from your registered email; we will delete it within 30 days, except records we must keep by law (such as payment receipts).

6. Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS. Passwords are stored hashed, and access to our systems is restricted to the Verimed team.

7. Children

Verimed is intended for medical students and graduates. It is not directed to children under 18.

8. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
